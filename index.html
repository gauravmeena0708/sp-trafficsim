ABOUT THIS MODEL

Explore an idea.
Then validate it.

Junction is an early planning prototype. Vehicles follow declared routes along explicit lanes through four-arm, T-junction or two-junction corridor layouts with fixed-time signals, or a single-lane roundabout where entries give way to circulating traffic. Approaches can have their own lanes, speeds, turning shares and right-turn pockets. Turning vehicles use the kerbside (left) or median (right) lane, and right turns yield to opposing traffic. Vehicles do not enter a junction unless the receiving lane has room, so queues can spill back between junctions. Mid-block lane changes, pedestrians and bus stops are not modelled.

A queue is a chain of vehicles slower than 2 m/s starting at the stop line, with no more than 10 m between them. Warm-up traffic remains in the simulation, but journey and accrued-delay measures use departures requested after warm-up.

Accrued delay compares elapsed movement with each vehicle’s desired speed, includes boundary waiting, and includes unfinished demand. No field calibration has been performed. Adaptive density-responsive timing, free-form network drawing and population-to-trip estimation are planned phases.
