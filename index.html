ABOUT THIS MODEL

Explore an idea.
Then validate it.

Junction is an early planning prototype. Vehicles follow explicit lanes through a four-arm junction with two fixed-time signal groups. Opposing traffic moves straight through; turning, crossing and lane-changing behaviour are not modelled.

Queue estimates use vehicles travelling below 2 m/s and include distance to the furthest slow vehicle. Warm-up traffic remains in the simulation, but journey and accrued-delay measures use departures requested after warm-up.

Accrued delay compares elapsed movement with each vehicle’s desired speed, includes boundary waiting, and includes unfinished demand. No field calibration has been performed. Adaptive density-responsive timing, editable connected networks, population-to-trip estimation and roundabouts are planned phases.
